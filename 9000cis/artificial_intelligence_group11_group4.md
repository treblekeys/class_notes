# Artificial Intelligence 
- Programmers tried to make a system that can follow scripts. 
    - Unfortunately, they realized that they need massive amount of datas rather than give AI scripts.
- From 2017 onwards, AI became big and started to do things that they would not have been able to do.
- AI is used everywhere, from business integration to daily lives to research and creativity.

## Ethics
- Uncompensated scraping.
    - AI is trained by taking or scraping data from sources around the world. All these data are taken without consent.

## Future
- AI will get more advanced in the future, such as making it multimodal and improving agents.
- AI will transform business with automation.
- It will also impact workforce such as jobs, collaboration, and AGI.

## LLMs vs Beyond LLMs
- LLMs are AI systems that are trained on extremely large datasets of text. 
- Beyond LLMs is the future of LLMs where AI will be able to handle complex reasoning and multimodal generation.
