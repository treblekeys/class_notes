# Civic Builders
- US nonprofit operating at the intersection of community development finance, real estate, and public edication infrastructure, financing and developing facilities for high-performing charter schools in underserved communites

## Quickbooks and Excel
- Civic Builders rely on quickbooks and Excel.
    - No multi-entity consolidation
    - Excel driven planning
    - Slow reporting cycles
    - Limited controls

## An Integrated Replacement Stack
- Move from quickbooks and Excel to **Oracle NetSuite**.
- This will address most of the issues that are presented with the current information system.
    - New system will have multi-entity consolidation
    - There are granular dimensions
    - Real time reporting
    - Stronger controls
    - Popeline visibility (salesforce)
    - Front to back integration

## Wher etransformation is incomplete
- Incomplete integration
- Persistent manual processes
- Limited cross functional visibility
- Data governace gaps 
- User adoption friction
- Transitional system risk

## Actions to close the gaps
- Complete system integration
- Eliminate redundant system
- Standardize and automate processes
- Centralized reporting layer
- Data governace framework
- Change management and training
