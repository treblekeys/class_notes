# Emerging Trends: Workforce Requirements and Skills

## Overview
- You will write a professional business assessment analyzing how one emerging
  trend discussed in class is likely to change workforce requirements and the
  skills needed for organizational success. Your analysis should demonstrate
  the ability to connect technology and business trends to human capital
  strategy, anticipate capability gaps, and recommend actionable steps that
  senior management could take to prepare the organization.

### Executive Summary
- Provide a concise overview of the emerging you selected, your expectations
  about how it will affect workforce requirements, and your primary
  recommendation for how an organization should respond.

### Worforce Impact Analysis
- Identify and explain the ways this trend will alter the nature of work
  (roles, tasks, and organization structures)
- Analyze how required skills, knowledge, and competencies are likely to evolve
  over the next 3-5 years.
- Examine which current roles are most at risk of disruption and which new
  roles or skill profiles are likely to emerge.
- Evaluate the implications for talent acquisition, development, and retention
  practices.

### Strategic Implications for Organizations
- Access how this trend will influence broader business and IT strategy (e.g.
  digital transformation priorities, operating model, sourcing decisions)
- Consider how existing organizational structures, performance metrics, and
  culture might either support or hinder adaptation to these new workforce
  requiements.
- Discuss the trade-offs between short-term efficiency (e.g. automation, cost
  reduction) and long-term workforce resilience and capability-building.

### Recommendations and Implementation Considerations
- Propose specific, actionable initiative (e.g. reskilling programs, new hiring
  profiles, partnerships, changes to performance management) that organizations
  should undertake to prepare their workforce for this trend.
- Identify potential barriers to implementation (such as budget constraints,
  cultural resistance, legacy systems, or skills gaps in HR/learning functions)
and strategies to overcome them.
- Suggest alternative or complementary approaches (for example, ecosystem
  partnerships, automation plus upskilling, or phased role redesign) to address
  anticipated workforce challenges. 
- Consider how your recommendations could be adapted to different
  organizational contexts (e.g. large vs. small firms, different industries,
  public vs. private sector).

### Technical Requirements
- **Length**: 600 - 1250 words
- **Audience**: Write for a professional business audience (equivalent to
  senior managers or executives)
- **Tone**: Professional, analytical, and objective
- **Citations**: Use proper academic citations when referencing specific data
  or claims from the case study
- **Format**: Use clear section headings corresponding to the required
  components
