# Validity, Accuracy, and Reputation
- Use case for Robotaxis
    - A solution to mobility challenges in urban and suburban areas
    - Can increase traffic safety, decrease emissions, and have cost advantages in removing drivers.
- The V-Model
    - Allows for parallel testing at each development phase, enabling earlier bug detection and quicker turnaround for fixing defects.
    - Use of SIL and HIL simulations have allowed companies to log billions of "virtual miles".
- Can be expanded to new markets like delivery services and underserved areas
- Lower the cost through the use of AI
- Challanges:
    - high upfront cost
    - need to improve profitability vs traditional taxis
    - safety concerns

## Reputation and misinformation risks through AI
- Main risks: misinformation, AI mistakes, system malfunctions
- How it affects AI's reputation: lacks of trust, low credibility, and negative perspective on companies
- Impact on everyday lives: mistrust in everyday lives, quantity of AI tools, and can cause reputational damages.
- One exmaple is when McDonald's AI chatbot cause data exposure due to weak security, not AI failure.
