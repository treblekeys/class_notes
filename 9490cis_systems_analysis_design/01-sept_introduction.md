## Chapter 1 lecture
**Information technology** combines hardware, software, and services to manage, communicate, and share information. Professionals should understand that technological advances are part of a long term process that
brings changes and innovation, but it never ends. 

**Systems analysis and design** is a process for developing high quality information systems. These
systems should combine technology with people and data to support business functions like order
processing, inventory control, human resources, etc.
A **system analyst** is a member of the IT department team who helps plan, develop, and maintain
information systems.

A **system** is a set of related components that produce specific results.
A **mission-critical system** is __vital__ to a company's operations.
**Data** are basic facts that are the system's raw material
**Information** is data that has been transformed into valuable output for users.

Information systems have 5 components:
- **Hardware** is everything in the physical layer of the information system
- **Software** are the programs that control the hardware and produce desired information or results
- **System software** manages the hardware components and includes the operating system, security
  software, device drivers, and utility programs
- **Data** is raw material, will be turned into useful information
- **Processes** describe the tasks and functions that users, managers, and IT staff perform to
  achieve specific results
- People who have interest in an information system are called **stakeholders**
