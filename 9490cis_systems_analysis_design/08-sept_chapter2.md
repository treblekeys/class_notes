# Chapter 2

Strategic planning is identifying long-term organizational goals, strategies, and resources.
SWOT: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats

Business case refers to a proposal's reasons or justifications. Why are we doing this project, what
is it about, how does this solution address key business issues, how much does it cost, how long
will it take, will be suffer a productivity loss during this transition, etc.
