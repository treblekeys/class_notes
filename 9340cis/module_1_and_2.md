## Why do we use databases?
- In the 1950s to 1990s, databases are used for data creations, IMS, network database management
- Some models began to form in the 1970s, such as the relational data model.
- Nowadays, databases are used in data science. 
    - They are used in data mining, data warehousing, web, etc.
    - Databases became very important because of the prevalence of the ==Internet==. 

## When to use database

**Information Hierarchy**
- From bottom to top:
    - Data, Information, Knowledge, Intelligence
- **Data**: It is the raw data that you get. 
    - For example, a customer makes an account for Costco. The customer's data is now available to Costco through this account. 
- **Information**: Data becomes valuable when it becomes information. Information is extracted from the data gathered.
    - Ex: when the customer shops at Costco, Costco is able to get information regarding that customer, such as what they buy, how much they pay, etc.
