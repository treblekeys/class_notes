Operating system is a set of programs that is an interface between the software and the hardware
and an interface between the computer and the user.

internet (lowercase) is a network of networks.

Internet (uppercase) is the massive, global network that connects everything together.

Extranet is when you let an outside person into your personal network and act as it it is internal.
It is a private network that shares part of an organization's information with external partners.

Intranet is the internal network. It is an internal repository of information and resources that is
accessed by people within the organization using standard web-based protocols.

LAN is local area network. WAN is wide area network. Difference is the proximity of the devices
connected. If people in the same campus, that's a LAN. If you contact someone from across the world,
it is a WAN. However, distance is only one item that determines the two. The second item is who owns
the communication. The third item is protocol being used (WIFI/Ethernet).

PAN is personal area network. It is a network centered around a person. Mouse connected the
computer, headphone connected to phone, etc. Centered around a person or their workspace.

CAN is a campus area network. It is like a LAN for an area like a school campus, military base,
corporate building, etc.

MAN is metropolitan area network. It covers a larger area than LAN and CAN but smaller than WAN.

Confidentiality is only those who should know, do know.
Integrity means the data is what the data should be.
Availability is data is available when it is needed.
